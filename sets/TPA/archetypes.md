# The Pale Age: Draft Archetypes

## {W}{U} Archive
Strategically recover spells from your graveyard to overcome opponent's threats.

## {U}{B} Suppress
Interfere with your opponent’s draws and counteract their most impactful plays.

## {B}{R} Spectacle
Ping and drain your opponents to make your spells cheaper. Remember to take a bow after dealing the final blow.

## {R}{G} Bloodthirst
Start attacking early, then turn each successful hit into larger and more dangerous threats.

## {G}{W} Swarm
Create an army of creature tokens and overwhelm your opponent through sheer numbers.

## {W}{B} Life and Death
Move everyone closer to death while preserving the slightest edge, so your opponent dies just before you do.

## {U}{R} Ichor
Generate Ichor tokens to fuel powerful abilities.

## {B}{G} Consume
Produce a mountain of disposable fodder, then feed it to your creatures to create massive threats and explosive turns.

## {R}{W} Trailblaze
Crew vehicles and saddle mounts each turn to assemble the ultimate value engine.

## {G}{U} Transfuse
Adapt to shifting board states by moving counters between your creatures or onto your opponent's creatures.
